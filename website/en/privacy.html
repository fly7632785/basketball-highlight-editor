Privacy Policy

Your videos stay on your device

Last updated: 2026-11-05

Short version: BHE processes your videos on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and we collect no personal data.

Video processing is local

Analysis, review and export all run on your device (computer or phone). Your video files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.

We do not collect personal data

BHE requires no account and never asks for your name, email, or contacts.

App data stays local

Projects, review history, settings and diagnostic logs are stored on your device, and you can delete them at any time.

Diagnostics are user-delivered

If you choose "Feedback", BHE copies a diagnostic report (app version, system info, logs) to your clipboard so you can paste it to us. Nothing is sent automatically.

Updates

The Mac App Store version is updated through the App Store and does not check for updates inside the app. The desktop and Android versions downloaded from this website contact our update server — without any user identifiers and without uploading your video — only when you tap "Check for updates" to fetch the latest version information.

Permissions

  • BHE only accesses the video files you choose and the export folder you pick;
  • it never reads other files without your action.

Children

BHE collects no personal data and is suitable for all ages.

Contact

Use the in-app "Feedback" feature, or open an issue on the GitHub repository.
