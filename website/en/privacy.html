Privacy Policy

Your videos stay on your device

Draft policy

BHE processes your videos on your device. We do not upload, see, or receive your game footage. The Mac App Store edition can send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and you can turn both off.

Video processing is local

Analysis, review and export run on your device. Your video files, file names and folder paths are never uploaded.

No account

BHE does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location.

Usage statistics

In the Mac App Store edition, Google Firebase Analytics counts app use through a fixed set of events: app opened, analysis started and finished, export started and finished, paywall shown, subscription started and finished, and purchase restored. Events include a few fixed labels, such as export mode or trial status.

Firebase also receives standard technical information it collects automatically, such as app version, device model, operating system version, language, and an app-instance ID that is not linked to your identity. Google may derive an approximate location from your IP address.

Crash reports

The Mac App Store edition uses Firebase Crashlytics. A report contains the error type, the length and a one-way hash of the error message, and the names of our own code modules with line numbers. It does not contain file names, folder paths, video content or error message text.

Your control and retention

In About, the Anonymous usage statistics switch turns both services off immediately for the current session. Turning it back on takes effect the next time you launch the app. Data is retained according to the default settings of our Firebase project, which we can adjust in the Firebase console.

Other editions

The direct-download and China editions do not include Firebase.

Diagnostics and subscriptions

Feedback copies a diagnostic report (app version, system information and logs) to your clipboard for you to paste to us. Nothing is sent automatically. Apple handles subscriptions; BHE never sees your payment details.

Children

BHE is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data.
