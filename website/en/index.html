Frequently asked questions

Does BHE upload my videos?

No. Analysis, review, and export happen on your device, and your video is not uploaded. The Mac App Store edition can send optional usage statistics and crash reports; see our privacy policy.

What if detection misses something?

Remove false positives, adjust clip boundaries, or add missed moments manually. You make the final call.

Does it support full-court footage?

Analysis currently works with a fixed camera and one hoop in view. Full-court footage with both ends is not supported.

Can I use it on iPhone?

iPhone currently supports playback, review, and export, but not analysis. Availability is coming soon.
