FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does BHE upload my videos?

No. Analysis, review, and export happen on your device. The iPhone and Mac App Store editions can send optional usage statistics and crash reports; see the privacy policy.

What if detection misses a moment?

Remove false positives, adjust clip boundaries, or add a missed moment manually. You make the final call.

Does it support full-court footage?

Not yet. Analysis works with a fixed camera and one hoop in view.

Can I use it on iPhone?

Yes. BHE is on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 16 or later). An Android version is in development.
