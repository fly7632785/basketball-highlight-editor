WORKFLOW

Five steps from a full game to a highlight reel

  1. 01Import the game video

    Choose one game file and the time range you want to analyze.

  2. 02Set the hoop area

    Start with automatic detection, then draw or fine-tune the area if needed.

  3. 03Let it scan

    Use Fast for a quick first pass, or Standard when completeness matters.

  4. 04Review the candidates

    Play through the list, remove false positives, and adjust the timing.

  5. 05Export the highlights

    Merge everything into one reel or export clips by player or tag.

FEATURES

Small feature set. All on the main path.

Find suspected scores

Scan fixed-camera, single-hoop game videos and turn a long timeline into a short list of moments worth checking.

Fast or Standard

Get a quick first draft with Fast, or spend more time on a fuller Standard pass. Review and export work the same way in both modes.

Tags for players and scenes

Create your own tags for players, threes, dunks, fouls, defense, or anything else you want to find later.

Two export styles

Merge the kept candidates into one reel, or export each candidate as its own clip. Open the output folder when the job is done.

Fill in what it missed

Add a manual candidate from the current video position when automatic detection misses a moment.

Private by default

No account or online API is required. The model and analysis run on your computer, keeping game footage where it belongs.

HONEST SCOPE

Where BHE works best today

Good fit

  • Fixed camera position, such as a tripod or a stable stand
  • One game with one hoop in the frame
  • School teams, pickup games, amateur leagues, and practice footage

Review carefully

  • Frequent camera movement or handheld tracking
  • The hoop is blocked for long stretches
  • Multiple hoops appear in the same frame

A candidate is a suspected event, not a final verdict. That is why the review workbench is at the center of BHE.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does BHE need an internet connection?

Analysis, preview, review, and export run locally and do not require an account. Internet is only needed to download the app or read online documentation.

Is it really free?

Yes. BHE is open source under the MIT License, with no charge by video length or export count. Models and media still have their own license terms.

What if the detection is wrong?

Candidates stay included by default. Remove false positives, adjust the boundaries, or add a missed moment manually. BHE gives you a draft, not an irreversible decision.

Is there an iOS version?

The iOS project and development path are in progress. There is no downloadable iOS build on the website yet.
